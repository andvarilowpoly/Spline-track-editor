User manual

Track Spline Generator

Turn a 2D sketch or a minimap image into a game-ready 3D race track, with terrain, hills, tunnels, water, vegetation and gameplay items, and export it to your engine.

  • Units meters, Z up
  • Exports GLB, FBX, Blender, 3ds Max, JSON, OBJ
  • Online trackspline.vercel.app

01What it does

Track Spline Generator builds a racing track from a 2D layout and dresses the scene around it. You give it a shape (a freehand stroke, a minimap image or one of the samples). It fits a smooth spline, works out heights, crossings and banking, and builds the road mesh. Then you add terrain, hills with tunnels, rivers and lakes, trees, grass, props, shadows, signs and gameplay items. When you are done, you export everything as one 3D scene for your engine.

Shape

Draw, trace an image or start from a sample. Edit control points, loops, helixes, fixed-radius curves and shortcuts.

Height

Automatic hills with hard limits on grade and vertical radius, or exact keyframes you set yourself.

Scene

Terrain, hills and tunnels, water, vegetation, decoration sets, shadow planes, signs and track items.

Deliver

Drive it with the game camera, then export GLB or FBX with colliders, nodes, triggers and named objects.

Almost every change rebuilds the track and the scene immediately, and Ctrl+Z undoes each gesture.

02Getting started

Opening the app

  • Online: open trackspline.vercel.app in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Nothing to install.
  • Locally: in the project folder run node server.js (Node 18 or newer) and open http://localhost:5173. Use PORT=5174 node server.js for another port, or double-click iniciar.sh, iniciar.command or iniciar.bat. With the local server, autosave can also write copies to the server folder.

Your first track in ten steps

  1. Get a shape. Choose Input → Sample, paste a minimap with Ctrl+V, use Load image…, or pick Draw and sketch on the map.
  2. Set the scale. In Layout, set Lap length (600–1500 m suits karts) and Track width (10–16 m).
  3. Place the finish line with Move the finish line… and choose the direction with Reverse direction.
  4. Refine the shape with Edit points: drag points, smooth stretches, set exact curve radii, add loops or a helix.
  5. Tune the heights in Elevation: amount of hills, seed and maximum grade. Check the elevation profile under the map.
  6. Add sections where needed: bridges with pillars or trenches with walls.
  7. Generate the scene with Generate terrain and Generate trees and decoration (3D view toolbar).
  8. Paint the landscape: hills (tunnels appear by themselves), water, sculpted relief.
  9. Add gameplay: nitro strips, coin stripes, turbo pads, puddles, signs, triggers and nodes.
  10. Drive and deliver: test with Game camera, check the warnings, then Save the project and Export → Scene (.glb).

03Key concepts

TermMeaning
Main routeThe track itself. It has a racing direction; s is the distance traveled from the finish line (s = 0).
Shortcut (alternative route)A route that leaves the main track and joins it again. It has its own width, textures and edges.
Lap lengthSets the scale of everything. The drawn shape is stretched so one lap measures that many meters.
Control pointsEditable points the spline passes through exactly. They appear when you use Edit points. A point can have a pinned height (orange in the profile).
SectionPart of the main route with its own settings: Track (terrain adapts), Bridge (terrain stays, pillars appear) or Trench (a cutting with walls).
CrossingWhere the track passes over itself or a shortcut. The app decides which pass goes on top and keeps the clearance.
Left and rightAlways measured by the racing direction: dirt roads, barriers, signs, tree sides. Reversing the direction rebuilds them.
PlaceholdersCones, cubes and planes you see in the editor stand in for your own art. Load FBX or GLB models to replace them.
Canvas unitsBrush strokes and points are stored in 2D map pixels and converted to meters with the scale. Change Lap length before painting hills, water and decoration.

08Edit points

Map toolbar · Edit points. Double-clicking the track from any tool enters it at the nearest point.

Working with points

  • Move: drag a point on the map, or click its sphere in 3D and use the gizmo (Free, XY plane, Z only).
  • Add: double-click the track. Delete: right-click, Alt+click or Del.
  • Select several: Shift+drag draws a box; Ctrl+click adds or removes one; Shift+click takes every point between the last one and this one. Works on the map, the profile and the 3D view.
  • Open (checkbox): deleting a point of a closed circuit leaves it open. A section between the two ends closes it again.

12Warnings panel

Under the profile. Errors (red) and warnings list problems with their position s, such as a grade above the maximum, a vertical radius below the minimum, missing clearance at a crossing, or a hairpin so tight the mesh would cross itself. Click one to jump there. Delete warnings (trash icon) hides the current ones; new or different ones still appear. A clean track shows "No problems".

Sections

A section is part of the main route with its own settings. With Edit points, select consecutive points and press Create section (or Section in the sub-toolbar).

TypeBehavior
TrackThe terrain adapts as on the rest of the track. Takes everything from the main track; you can change width, lateral offset and textures.

20Saving and autosave

  • Save downloads <project name>.tsg.json with everything: shape, heights, scene, textures and models. Open loads it back.
  • Autosave (Settings) keeps copies every few minutes when something changed:
    • always in this browser;
    • in the server folder when you run the app with node server.js;
    • also in a folder of your choice in Chrome or Edge (Choose folder…; after reopening the app press Grant permission once).
  • Save a copy now and Open autosave… (a list with thumbnail and date) are in the same place.

23Tips

  • Decide scale and direction early. Changing Lap length rescales everything, and reversing the direction rebuilds sides, signs and river flow.
  • Curves: keep the tightest radius around 1.5 times the track width; tighter hairpins raise warnings. Use Radius for clean arcs and Smooth for shaky strokes.
  • Heights: 10 % maximum grade is a safe default; 12–15 % is the arcade limit. Keep the finish line flat. A crossing needs the clearance plus the deck (about 7 m).
  • Shortcuts should leave and rejoin tangent to the main track. A good one is shorter but harder.
  • Free scenery: a hill over the track gives a tunnel; a river under a bridge looks great; signs and edges help players read the track.
  • Performance: watch the triangle counter, use Optimized track geometry, lower terrain density, use Line mode and higher outline detail for rivers, and keep Single mesh on for trees and grass.
  • Something missing? Check that its section is turned on, then use Search.
  • Before delivering: drive it, read the warnings panel, save the project and export.
